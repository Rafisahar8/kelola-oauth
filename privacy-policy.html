Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Kelola is a project financial management application designed to help users manage budgets, transactions, and financial documentation.

Information We Collect

Kelola may collect account information, project details, financial records, transaction descriptions, and documents uploaded by users.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide application features, manage project finances, maintain account security, and synchronize financial reports.

Google Drive Integration

If users enable Google Drive integration, Kelola uses the authorized Google Drive access to store and synchronize financial reports and related documents.

Kelola does not use Google Drive data for advertising or sell users' personal information.

Data Security

Kelola uses authentication and access controls to protect user information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated information through the application administrator. Some records may be retained where required by applicable law.

Third-Party Services

Kelola uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and application services, as well as Google Drive when users authorize its integration.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Kelola application administrator.
